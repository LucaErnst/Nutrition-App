`calorie`, `macro` und `tracker` stehen schon im Untertitel; Apple liest Name, Untertitel und
Keywords zusammen. So werden „calorie counter", „food diary" und „macro counter" gefunden
(Vorschlag vom 10.10.2026, docs/RELEASE-1.2.md B3).

Bis 1.1: `calorie,macro,tracker,protein,barcode,scanner,offline,diet,bulk,cut,water,weight,coach,calisthenics` –
99 Zeichen. `nutrition` ist gestrichen: es steht schon im App-Namen und wird dort ohnehin indexiert –
so passt `offline` hinein, der einzige echte Unterschied zu Lifesum und Lose It!. (Die alte Zeile war
101 Zeichen und damit über dem Limit.)

## Beschreibung – Englisch

Serious Nutrition is a calorie and macro tracker for people who train with a goal. Built by a calisthenics coach for his own bulk and cut phases – no account, no ads, no data leaving your phone.

TRACK IN SECONDS
• Thousands of basic foods are built into the app – meat, fish, dairy, grains, vegetables, fruit, nuts, oils – searchable without internet. In English, that includes 6,300 American foods from the USDA
• About 290,000 popular branded products from around the world are built in too – scanning and product search work instantly, even offline
• Swiss dishes included: Älplermagronen, Birchermüesli, Cervelat, Sbrinz, Wähe – with official Swiss values
• Anything not built in is looked up online in Open Food Facts (3+ million products)
• Log eggs, bananas or apples by the piece – the weight is filled in
• Your own foods come first: whatever you scan or type once is saved and sits at the top next time
• Amount stepper, quick portions like “1 handful” or “last 45 g”
• Templates: save a breakfast once, add it with one tap
• Copy yesterday’s meal, undo a delete, quick entry with calories only

TARGETS THAT FIT TRAINING
• Separate calories for training days and rest days
• Protein minimum, fat range, carbs as the remainder
• Needs calculator: sex, age, height, weight, everyday activity and training days – with a clear breakdown of how your target is built, any time under More
• Phases with start and end date: bulk, cut, maintenance

THINK IN WEEKS
• Weekly budget: a day over target is fine as long as the week adds up
• Weekly average, target comparison and a plain-language summary
• Steps from Apple Health explain a stalling trend – they never change your targets
• Water tracking with a daily goal, +250 / +500 ml

WEIGHT TREND
• Daily weight with a 7-day moving average, so daily swings don’t fool you

YOUR UNITS
• Grams and kilograms, millilitres, centilitres and litres
• Ounces and fluid ounces (US and UK), pounds, stones, feet and inches
• Body weight and height in the units you actually think in

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything is stored on your device, no login required
• Export and import backups as a file
• Follows your device language: English and German

Serious Nutrition Pro unlocks templates, portion sizes, the weight chart, widgets and Apple Health – one payment, no subscription, no trial that turns into one.
