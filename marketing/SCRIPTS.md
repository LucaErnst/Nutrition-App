# Recording scripts – weeks 1–2

Speak to the camera, English, your own words are fine as long as the claim stays. Each take 8–15 s
(≈ 25–40 words). Say the first sentence **immediately** – no "hey guys", no intro. Record 2–3 takes
per script, phone upright, eye level. Days 3, 6 and 10 are app-only (no recording needed).

## Week 1

**Day 1 – "I built my athletes an app."** (Story)
> I coach calisthenics. And every tracker I gave my athletes was the same: subscription, ads, one calorie number for every single day. So I built my own. It's called Serious Nutrition. It's free. And I'll show you why it's different.

**Day 2 – "Stop counting daily calories."** (Opinion)
> One day over target ruins nothing. A whole week over target – that's what ruins your cut. So stop panicking about Tuesday. My app counts the week as a budget. Tuesday's over? Fine. Wednesday's a bit lighter. Week done.

**Day 4 – "Your scale lies. Every morning."** (Opinion)
> You're up one kilo overnight. That's not fat. That's water, salt, and the rice you ate last night. Daily weight is noise. What matters is the seven-day trend – and that's the only line I look at.

**Day 5 – "Training day ≠ rest day."** (Opinion)
> Why would you eat the same on a rest day as after two hours of training? You wouldn't. So your targets shouldn't either. Training day: more. Rest day: less. One tap, and the whole day adjusts.

**Day 7 – "No account. No ads. No subscription. Why?"** (Story)
> Because as a coach, I don't need your data. And you shouldn't have to hand it over to log your breakfast. Everything stays on your phone. Want to switch phones? Export a file. Done. That's it.

## Week 2

**Day 8 – Reply: "How much protein do I really need?"** (Reply – if no comment asks, use this anyway)
> One point six to two point two grams per kilo of bodyweight. That's it. Eighty kilos? A hundred thirty to a hundred seventy-five. The app does this maths for you in the first two minutes – and then it just shows you what's left today.

**Day 9 – "Same breakfast every day? Then don't type it 100 times."** (Opinion)
> If tracking takes you five minutes a day, you'll stop in a week. Save your breakfast once as a template. Tomorrow it's one tap. Eating what you ate yesterday? Copy yesterday. Tracking should take seconds, not willpower.

**Day 11 – "My bulk, week 6: the numbers."** (Story – use your real numbers if you like)
> Week six of my bulk. Weekly average: two thousand seven hundred sixty calories, a hundred seventy-eight grams of protein. Trend: plus zero point seven kilos this month. That's the pace I want. Slower than that, I'd add carbs. Faster, I'd pull back. No guessing.

**Day 12 – "I don't have time to track."** (Opinion, with a wink)
> "I don't have time to track." You have time for three hours of TikTok. Five seconds per meal is fine. Scan, tap, done. You don't lack time. You lack a tool that doesn't waste it.

**Day 13 – Comment question of the week** (Reply – pick the most common question from days 1–10; fallback below)
> A lot of you asked: "Does it work without internet?" Yes. Everything's on your phone. The only thing that needs a connection is scanning a barcode you've never scanned before. Once it's in, it stays in.

**Day 14 – "Reminders that don't nag."** (Opinion)
> Most apps ping you ten times a day until you mute them. Mine sends max three – and if you've already logged lunch, it doesn't remind you about lunch. Reminders should respect you. That's how it should be.

## Delivery
- Energy: calm, direct, a bit dry. You're stating facts, not selling.
- Look into the lens on the first sentence. That's the hook; the text overlay carries the same words.
- Pauses are fine – I cut them. Stumbles: just restart the sentence, keep rolling.
- Don't say the app name in every clip. Days 1, 7 and 11 are enough; the outro card does the rest.
