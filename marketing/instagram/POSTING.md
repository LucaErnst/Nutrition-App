**Karussell F – How much protein?**

1.6–2.2 g per kg body weight. That's the number.

80 kg? Aim for 128–176 g a day, spread over 3–5 meals. Serious Nutrition does the maths and shows what's left today.

General guidance for healthy adults who train – not medical advice.
