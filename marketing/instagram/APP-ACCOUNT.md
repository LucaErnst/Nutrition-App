**F – How much protein? (Mo 19.10.)**

1.6 to 2.2 g per kg body weight. That's the number.

80 kg? Aim for 128 to 176 g a day, spread over 3 to 5 meals. Serious Nutrition does the maths and shows what's left today.

General guidance for healthy adults who train, not medical advice.

#protein #musclegain #nutritiontips #macros #calisthenics
