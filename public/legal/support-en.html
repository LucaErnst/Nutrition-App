Frequently asked

Where is my data? Only on your device. Export a backup under More → Backup before switching phones, then import it on the new one.

A product is missing or wrong. Product data comes from Open Food Facts. Enter it manually (Add → Manual) or correct it in your food database; you can also improve Open Food Facts itself.

The camera asks for permission every time (web version). Open the app URL in Safari → “aA” → Website Settings → Camera: Allow – or use the native app.
