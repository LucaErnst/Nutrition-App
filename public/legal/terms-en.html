2. What the app does

Serious Nutrition is a tool to log food, water and body weight and to compare them with targets you set. It is available as a web app and as an app for iOS and Android.

3. No medical advice

The app provides general nutrition tracking and calculations based on common formulas (for example Mifflin-St Jeor). It is not a medical device and does not provide medical, dietary or therapeutic advice. Suggested targets are estimates. Consult a physician or qualified nutrition professional before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, have an eating disorder or any medical condition. Do not use the app as a substitute for professional care.

4. Product data

Product names and nutrition values retrieved via barcode or search come from Open Food Facts, a collaborative database maintained by volunteers, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL). This data may be incomplete or wrong. Check values against the packaging; you can correct them in your own food database.
