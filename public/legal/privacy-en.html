Privacy Policy

Serious Nutrition · last updated 19 September 2026

1. Who is responsible

Luca Ernst, Wittlingerstrasse 142, 4058 Basel, Switzerland, beseriouscalisthenics@gmail.com (the “provider”, “we”).

2. The short version

  • Serious Nutrition stores everything you enter – meals, foods, water, weight, targets – only on your device.
  • There is no account, no login and no server of ours that receives your data.
  • We use no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • When you scan a barcode or search for a product, the barcode or search term is sent to Open Food Facts, an independent, non-profit food database.

3. Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following data in its local storage (IndexedDB in the browser, or the app container on iOS/Android):

  • food entries (food, amount, nutrition values, date, meal), your food database, templates and favorites;
  • water entries, weight entries, targets/phases, training days, language and other settings;
  • an optional local error log (technical error messages, no personal content) to help diagnose problems;
  • body data you enter in the onboarding (age, sex, height, weight, activity) are used once to calculate a suggestion and are not stored except the weight entry.

This data never leaves your device unless you export a backup yourself (see 6) or your device’s own backup system (iCloud, Google) copies app data as part of a device backup, which is governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policies.

5. Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a barcode. No photos or video are stored or transmitted; only the decoded barcode number is used.

6. Backups you create

You can export all your data as a JSON file and store it wherever you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, e‑mail). Where you send that file is your decision and outside our control. The native app additionally writes a daily backup file into its own documents folder on your device.

7. Health-related data

Nutrition and weight entries are health-related personal data. Because they are processed only on your device, no one but you has access to them. The app does not provide medical advice; see the Terms of Use.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children – and, as described above, we do not collect personal data at all.

9. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data. Because all data is stored on your device, you exercise these rights directly in the app: view and edit everything in the diary and under “More”, export under “More → Backup”, and delete everything by removing the app or clearing the browser’s site data. For questions contact beseriouscalisthenics@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example if optional cloud sync is added). The current version is always available at this address and inside the app under “More”.
